Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days. Express delivery arrives in 1–2 business days.
What is your return policy?
We accept returns within 30 days of delivery, provided the item is unworn and in its original packaging.
Do you offer Cash on Delivery?
Yes, Cash on Delivery is available at checkout in select areas, alongside mobile payment, bank transfer and card payment options.
How do I find my size?
Each product page includes a size guide link. If you're between sizes, we generally recommend sizing up.
Can I change or cancel my order?
Contact our support team as soon as possible after placing your order. Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be changed.
Are the products on this site real inventory?
This site is a demonstration storefront. Product, order and customer data shown here is sample content for illustrating the shopping experience.
